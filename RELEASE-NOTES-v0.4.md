## What changed since v0.3.1 (2026-09-27 → 2026-10-15)

### Dataset

- **AI evaluators.** The firms and people who test frontier models before release: Scale AI,
  Transluce, FAR.AI, Andon Labs, and their leaders, with the employment and board edges that tie
  evaluators to the labs they evaluate. Every new person carries two sources (six records gained a
  second source to pass the audit).
- **Records brought current on primary sources:** Hoan Ton-That off the Clearview board
  (April 2025); Alex Stamos at Cognition; Jan Leike; Paul Christiano at ARC and the OpenAI
  Foundation board; Audrey Tang; Nick Bostrom; Anna Makanju; and the people and organizations
  around the UN AI-panel nominations.
- **Source typing:** first-party sources are typed `official`, the audit's own vocabulary.

### Tooling

- **`server/data/profile-aliases.json`.** Graph node ids whose evilrobots.lol profile lives under a
  different slug (22 ids, among them `JVance`, `AtlanticCouncil`, `Kissinger`, `Carney`, `Blinken`,
  `BSmith`, `Aspen`). Read by `scripts/export_atlas.py`, so atlas and graph links resolve to the
  published profile.
- **`scripts/gen_readme_counts.py`** now writes the counts block to the README and the newest
  release notes only; earlier notes keep the numbers of the cut they describe.

## Reproducibility

This is a longitudinal release. Pin the tag to reproduce this cut; the series of cuts is itself the
bias-drift instrument. Every person record cites at least two primary sources, every Wikidata
identifier is verified against its record (`scripts/verify_qids.py --check`), and CI gates both.
