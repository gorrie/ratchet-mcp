## Identifier correction (2026-09-26) — re-pin anything that used a v0.2 ID

Every Wikidata QID in the dataset (404 distinct) was checked against the Wikidata API by label,
description and instance-of (P31 = human for persons). **255 were wrong**, most pointing at
unrelated items; 246 are replaced with the verified item and 9 removed where no matching item
exists. Every Wikipedia title (906) was checked against the MediaWiki API: 44 were titles that
never existed, identifier-style placeholders, or disambiguation / wrong-subject pages, and are
replaced or removed. One source that could not be found at its publisher (a Senate Judiciary
transcript) is removed. `audit_citations.py` passes; derived graph and atlas data regenerated.

### Measurement fidelity and gates (2026-07-26 → 08-04)

- **Edge relation vocabulary:** the adapter now carries `rel`; edges remapped to the canonical
  vocabulary. The 810 untyped edges were **measured at 26% precision** for automatic typing
  (2026-08-02) — so the answer is no; they stay untyped and the gap is published on THE
  RECORD's `#/gaps` rather than filled by a script.
- **Graph-sync integrity tests** (anti-drop / orphan / edge-parity) after the public
  revolving-door graph was synced to the 550-person dataset and the sync script fixed.
- **Drift gates** in the series-level `tools/freshness_gate.py`: atlas-sync, graphjs-sync,
  asserted-counts (docs saying "117 people" against 550), closed-vocab, play-shapes,
  field-shapes, near-dupes, liveness-age. `backstop` mis-tagging corrected (64% mis-fit → 0);
  two plays minted (`bar`, `ward`).
- **Citation liveness:** full sweep; 403/401 reclassified as bot-blocked-alive (government links
  were being false-flagged); 111 items queued to the real-network box (`docs/M5-QUEUE.md`).
- **Receipt capture:** redirect-blind Wayback lookup fixed; lawfare receipts pinned.
- **Detector span-offset bug** fixed and the never-swept `institutional_permeation` cues
  swept — the book self-audit had been inflated 2.1×.
- **CI:** the 100% coverage gate restored on both ratchet-mcp and tradecraft (2026-07-14 — the
  red had been environment bugs and a ghost file, not coverage); `mcp` SDK 2.x supported

## Provenance / discipline (unchanged)

- Every person ≥2 primary sources; every institution ≥1; `audit_citations.py` gates it.
- Positions-only `role`; no motive imputation, no characterizations, no private-life fields.
- Closed vocabularies for plays and actors; the texts lane grades per lens with receipts,
  never blended, never a verdict.
